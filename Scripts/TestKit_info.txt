### What this single file **tests / benches / checks**

* **Core Unit Tests (CPU & GPU):**

  * `Tests/core/test_params.py` — parameter loading/validation.
  * `Tests/core/test_icnn.py` — ICNN basics on CPU.
  * `Tests/core/test_dynamics.py` — full operator-split dynamics (fast marker + full).
  * `Tests/core/test_icnn_directgrad.py` — GPU direct-grad ICNN path.
  * `Tests/core/test_dynamics_batched_fused.py` — GPU fused/batched hot path.
  * `Tests/core/test_core_init.py
  * `Tests/core/test_kernels_placeholder.py

* **Non-Core Tests:**

  * `Tests/ops` — ops/thermostat, helpers.
  * `Tests/verifier` — receipts/verification utilities.
  * `Tests/output` — output/receipt formatting & integrity.

* **Profile Equivalence & Determinism:**

  * FAST / BALANCED **close** checks (HotLoopFused vs HotLoopGraph).
  * STRICT **bitwise** determinism + **sha256 hash equality** (sets `CUBLAS_WORKSPACE_CONFIG` and deterministic algos).

* **Benchmarks (with logging):**

  * **Fused & Unfused** paths for FAST, BALANCED, STRICT.
  * Graph capture (`--graph`) vs eager.
  * Iteration counts adapt to `--quick`.

* **Soak Tests:**

  * 3-minute stability runs (60s with `--quick`) for FAST/BALANCED/STRICT.
  * Intended to keep **VRAM slope ≈ 0** and watch latency p95 thresholds (printed by your soak script).

* **Receipts Sign/Verify (if keys present):**

  * Uses `Runtime/Config/keys/ed25519_private.pem` and `.../ed25519_public.pem` to create & verify a snapshot receipt.

* **Thermostat Watchdog:**

  * Ensures Thermostat can log alerts to `Runtime/Logs/alerts.log` and the wiring is alive.

* **API Import Smoke:**

  * Confirms `API.main` imports successfully (module initialization).

* **Linters & Types (informational):**

  * `ruff check .` (non-fatal; logs to `ruff.out/err`).
  * `mypy Core` (non-fatal; logs to `mypy.out/err`).
  * `types-PyYAML` and `nvidia-ml-py` helper stubs auto-install.

* **Nightly (optional):**

  * `--nightly` adds long-run tests marked `-m nightly -k long_run`.

* **Artifacts & Reporting:**

  * Every suite run writes **separate stdout/stderr files** per task under `Runtime/Logs/testkit_<timestamp>/`.
  * Final **PASS/FAIL summary** in `summary.txt` + console echo.
