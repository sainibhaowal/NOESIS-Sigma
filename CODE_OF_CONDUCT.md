# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NOESIS-Σ aims to be a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming research project. We welcome
participation regardless of experience, identity, background, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence, code, and documentation rather than people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, limitations, and prior work.
- Protect private information and do not publish credentials, private datasets, or private
  communications.
- Accept good-faith corrections and keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention,
deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and any other conduct that would make
participation unsafe or unreasonable are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the [Ravinder Singh GitHub profile](https://github.com/sainibhaowal).
Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. The maintainer will review reports in good faith,
protect confidentiality where practical, and may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the project community.

This code of conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation,
project-controlled channels, and project-related events.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
