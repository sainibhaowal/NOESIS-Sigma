# Governance

NOESIS-Σ / SigmaStorm is currently an independent, solo-maintainer research project hosted on
GitHub. The canonical project source, issues, pull requests, releases, and discussions are managed
through the GitHub repository.

## Project leadership

**Ravinder Singh** is the creator, principal author, maintainer, and release decision-maker for the
project. The maintainer owns the project roadmap, reviews changes, manages releases, and decides
whether proposed contributions fit the research direction.

Adding this governance document does not transfer copyright, patent rights, or project ownership.

## Decision process

- Small fixes may be merged by the maintainer after review and required checks pass.
- Design, API, architecture, licensing, and research-claim changes require maintainer approval.
- The maintainer may request evidence, tests, documentation, or a narrower scope before merging.
- The maintainer may reject or close proposals that are outside the project scope, unsafe, or not
  adequately reproducible.

## Contributions

Contribution guidance is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). There is currently no separate
Contributor License Agreement or copyright-assignment requirement. Accepted contributions are
distributed consistently with the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE), while contributors remain
responsible for having the right to submit their work.

## Releases and changes

The maintainer controls version numbers, release tags, changelog entries, and publication of release
artifacts. Research claims must remain tied to reproducible scripts, configurations, and recorded
evidence.

## Future evolution

If the project gains regular collaborators or a larger community, this document may be expanded to
define additional maintainers, review roles, decision procedures, and conflict-resolution practices.
